the swatch

A corner of a cloth from João's loom. Weave the same cloth: any board that makes this corner counts. Six weaves. The colours are plain to see — how many strings there are, and which string wears which colour, is the riddle. Beware: a colour may ride more than one string.

Recent weaves, browsable by how many strings they took. Once a year, João picks one weave from this wall and makes it real — cotton, no stitches, no seams, posted to whoever drew it.
