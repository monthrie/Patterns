## Researchers — high priority

**Matthew Yuan** (Oxford CS, DPhil student, Reuben College) — wrote the 2020 "Polyomino Loops" essay that poses the exact open problem; it ends *"keep me in the loop."* That's the opener. Send him the polyomino-loop census (`docs/polyomino-loop-census.md` — answers to his Q2 distribution question, the staircase max-L result, the OEIS-candidate sequence) plus João. Identity caveat: the Oxford profile matches name + Princeton-maths background, strong but circumstantial — the census content itself will confirm we have the right person. Also a potential buyer: the ideal gift/sale object is a woven prime polyomino (L=1) with its design code on the label.

**Radmila Sazdanović** (NC State, Professor — recently promoted) — co-author of "Mirror-Curves and Knot Mosaics"; active in math-art (Faculty LEAD, Bridges community). Angle: her universality result, woven by someone who never read it.

**Ljiljana Radović** (U. Niš, Full Professor, vice-dean for science) — Jablan's PhD student and the living torch-bearer of the mirror-curve programme. Both emails (institutional + gmail) verified on her CV PDF on the live university domain. Angle: João's irregular-board census extends the Jablan–Radović mirror curves to arbitrary polyominoes; she is the person most likely to *immediately* understand the jacket.

**Józef Przytycki** (GWU, Professor) — billiard-knots co-originator, organizes Knots in Washington (52/53 in 2026, per the department's own newsletter — conferences are a venue for João's story, not just a person). **Christoph Lamm** (independent, Wiesbaden) — billiard knots and symmetric unions, still publishing solo on arXiv (revised April 2025). He is himself an independent researcher outside academia — likely the most sympathetic reader of an outsider's discovery on this list.

**Pierre-Vincent Koseleff** (Sorbonne, IMJ-PRG) — co-proved "every knot is a billiard knot." Email deobfuscated from his own page ("[à]" form), current team member. **Use him as the route to Daniel Pecker** (co-author, apparently retired, page bot-blocked, no verifiable contact) — write to Koseleff and ask him to forward.

**sarah-marie belcastro + Carolyn Yackel** (MathILy/Bryn Mawr; Mercer) — the mathematical-fiber-arts community founders, co-editors of the **new journal *Interlace: A Journal of Mathematics and Fiber Arts*** (launching ~2026).
