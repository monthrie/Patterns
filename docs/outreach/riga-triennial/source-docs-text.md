## THE 8th RIGA INTERNATIONAL TEXTILE AND FIBRE ART TRIENNIAL — Tradition & Innovation
**Theme: LIMINAL SPACE** · Organizer: Latvian National Museum of Art / Museum of Decorative Arts and Design, Riga. Venue: Latvian National Museum of Art (1 Janis Rozentals Square, Riga, LV-1010). **28 May – 19 September 2027.**

**Theme:** *liminal* from Latin *limen* = border, threshold: an intermediate state in which change and transformation occur, neither the previous nor the next. Three suggested angles: (1) development of textile art and changes in the industry / where are the boundaries; (2) liminal space as a symbol of the modern world / global uncertainty, identity; (3) conversation with self in time and art / creative transition through texture, material, knot and thread.

### Key regulations
- **No participation fee.** International jury, anonymous judging 6–7 Nov 2026; results by end Nov 2026 by email.
- **Art. 7:** open to professional textile artists AND artists in other fields interested in textile/fiber. Conceptual works in any technique (tapestry, mixed, author's techniques, fiber-art objects, installations). Video textiles allowed, independent or part of an artwork.
- **Art. 8:** no limit on techniques/materials; priority to innovative treatment of the theme.
- **Art. 9:** one work per artist, recent (**2025–2027**), not displayed in large international shows before.
- **Art. 10:** max 3×3×3 m; preferably frameless; ready to display.
- **Art. 11:** judged from photos (300 dpi, jpg/tiff) sent via WeTransfer. Required files: completed Entry Form; full-size work photo (publication quality); detail photo (publication quality); artist photo (publication quality); brief CV (Word, ~1500 chars / ½ page); concept (Word, 500–800 chars, publication-ready English). Video-textile applications send the finished video file (≤5 min).
- **Art. 13:** deadline **1 September 2026**, via WeTransfer to **ieva.zvejniece@lnmm.gov.lv** AND **vita.birzaka@lnmm.gov.lv**.
- **Art. 14–15:** participation grants right to publish in catalogue + media; each selected artist gets one catalogue.
- **Art. 16–20:** selected works shipped door-to-door at artist's cost, NO customs duties, insured by museum during show; sent ready to hang with display instructions.
- Contacts: ieva.zvejniece@lnmm.gov.lv (+371 67830914); vita.birzaka@lnmm.gov.lv (+371 67357535); www.lnmm.lv.

### CV guidelines
Order: Name, Surname, Date of birth · Country · Address · Education and professional occupation (max 3 lines) · Most recent major selected exhibitions (max 5) · Awards (recent) · Works in collections (most prominent). Fill on computer (no scans), Word, ½–1 page, ~1500 chars.

### Concept guidelines
Main idea in 3–5 sentences, good publication-ready English, on computer (no scans), Word, **500–800 chars**. Too-long concepts will be shortened.

### Entry form fields
Surname; First name; Pseudonym (if any); Date of birth; Represented country; Address; Postcode; City; Country; Tel; E-mail. About artwork: Title (English); Year of creation; Material and technique; Size (height × width × depth). Attach: CV, visual info, concept. Agreement to rules + catalogue/advertising publication.
