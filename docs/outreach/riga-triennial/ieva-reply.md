# Ieva Zvejniece's reply (received 2026-06, verbatim)

Curator of Collections, Latvian National Museum of Art — to William.

---

Dear William,

Thank you very much for your email and for introducing me to João's remarkable work. I must admit that I have never encountered anything quite like this before. It is a fascinating and highly original approach to textile construction.

Based on what I have seen, João's work appears to fit the scope of the 8th Riga International Textile and Fibre Art Triennial, and I would warmly encourage him to submit an application. Like all artists, his work will be evaluated by the international selection jury alongside all other submissions.

Since the uniqueness of João's work lies not only in the finished objects but also in the technique itself, I would recommend presenting the submission as one coherent project, illustrated with several photographs. In addition to overall views of the work, I would encourage including close-up images that clearly show the knot structure and construction, as well as photographs illustrating the making process if possible.

The written description will also be particularly important. I would strongly recommend explaining the technique in detail, highlighting what makes it unique, and describing the creative process behind the work. This will help the selection jury better understand the originality and significance of João's practice.

I hope he decides to submit an application, and I wish him every success.

Kind regards,
IEVA ZVEJNIECE
Curator of Collections
