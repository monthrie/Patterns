## The craft, in his words

- **"It always joins to itself… I cut the same strings. And this I use with needles… so you won't notice it. Yeah. It's easy."** — `…booklet-circuits` [02:48]. The invisible join, explained: small cut pieces of the same string, sewn back with needles. ("It's easy" — the most casual description of the craft's most astonishing feature.)
- **"You gotta do it first… that's why now I only do first things here."** — `…booklet-circuits` [07:02]. Simulation before cotton. (⚠ verify "here" = the app on his phone or the booklet — video shows which.)
- **"Instead of four I put three."** — `…booklet-circuits` [06:17]. The experimental method: change one parameter, see what the rule does.
- **"It has to be factors of four. Four or eight or twelve."** — `…booklet-circuits` [04:06]. His empirical colour/circuit rule for that shape family.
- **"When I'm going to stitch this… now I have colours, and the colours gotta match."** — `…booklet-circuits` [08:34]. The seam constraint: panel colours must align — assembly is colour-topology too.
- **"You want them to be enough to fulfil the three layers… twelve big pieces or eight big pieces around. You've got to be focused, so you won't get to a point that's helpless."** — `…craft-demo-paper` [01:28]. The 3-pass densify ON TAPE + the planning burden + "a point that's helpless."
- **"There's less walking around with the material from one side to the other… much less. That means the job can be done easier."** — `…craft-demo-paper` [01:04]. The difficulty inversion (more loops = shorter walks), in his words; Wilmon on tape: "interesting, because it's actually more complex."
- **"Since I've been putting my hands on this again, I've been learning some stuff. Ways of proceeding."** — `…craft-demo-paper` [02:18]. Still discovering technique (then describes the sleeve/cloth trick for 3D threading).
- **"This is the discovery."** — `…booklet-circuits` [03:23]. THE original accident piece, on camera. (⚠ He compares its dark colourway to a swastika — the piece reads as a bold pinwheel; the site must never use that comparison; photograph in its other colourways.)
- **"24, 32. The next one will be 48, 64. Let's see if it works… I think it will stay with eight."** — `…booklet-circuits` [08:47]. Doubling dimensions + a live prediction (the scaling law, lived). Testable in the app — do it and tell him.
- **"All right, everybody. Can you see me? There you are. That's the way people work."** — `…craft-demo-paper` [03:26], to camera. The teacher's instinct, unprompted. For the Aprender section.
