## Notable discoveries (feed into knowledge-base / specimen manifest)
- **He works in natural jute/twine for open-mesh pieces** (vests, nets) as well as coloured cotton — a whole material register we hadn't recorded.
- **A net with a deliberate circular hole** — boards with holes are exactly the open-problem case; there's a physical piece of it.
- **Product range is wide**: scarves, vests, mittens, slippers, bands with wooden toggle closures, mats, runners — not just cushions/jacket/shoes.
- **He uses paper templates with numbered nail positions** taped to boards (process shots) — design-to-board transfer method, ask him about it.
- **The workshop is a caravan/van interior** — spool shelf, drying racks, weaves on the bed. Atmospheric, honest; great for the "Two crafts" section.
- **A needle/rod tool** is used to pass cord through dense lattice (process + worktable shots).
