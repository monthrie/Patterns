# References — sona, mirror curves, billiard knots, and the related traditions

Verified bibliography for the João das Cordas website (`/matematica` reference apparatus) and for any future paper/Bridges submission. Compiled 2026-06-10 by web research; each link was fetch-verified unless flagged. Companion: `where-this-craft-sits-in-mathematics.md` (the essay these support), `knowledge-base.md` §3–4.

> Link-status legend: ✓ fetch-verified · ◐ confirmed via search listings / mirrors, click once before publishing · ✗ no online source found (library/bookseller only).

## 1. Sona & Paulus Gerdes

- ✓ **Gerdes, P. — *Sona Geometry from Angola: Mathematics of an African Tradition* (Polimetrica, 2006).** The definitive English monograph: monolinearity, symmetry classes, reconstruction of the tradition, the gcd theorem. No legal full text online (Polimetrica defunct); [front matter/TOC scan (GBV Göttingen)](https://www.gbv.de/dms/goettingen/52505197X.pdf) · buy print via [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/geometry-Angola-Mathematics-African-Tradition/dp/8876990550)/AbeBooks · [Gerdes' Lulu storefront](https://www.lulu.com/spotlight/pgerdes).
- ✓ **Gerdes, P. — *Lunda Geometry: Mirror Curves, Designs, Knots, Polyominoes, Patterns, Symmetries* (Lulu, 2008).** Where mirror curves abstracted from sona become mathematics — the direct ancestor of the polyomino-loop question. [Lulu](https://www.lulu.com/shop/paulus-gerdes/lunda-geometry-mirror-curves-designs-knots-polyominoes-patterns-symmetries/paperback/product-3078236.html) · [Apple Books](https://books.apple.com/us/book/lunda-geometry/id442124473).
- ✓ **Gerdes, P. — *Drawings from Angola: Living Mathematics* (colour ed. 2012, Lulu).** Child-friendly introduction to the sona storytelling tradition. [Lulu](https://www.lulu.com/shop/paulus-gerdes/drawings-from-angola-living-mathematics-color-edition/paperback/product-21617238.html).
- ✓ **Gerdes, P. — *African Pythagoras* (colour ed. 2011, Lulu).** [Lulu](https://www.lulu.com/shop/paulus-gerdes/african-pythagoras-a-study-in-culture-and-mathematics-education/paperback/product-15231828.html).
- ◐ **The mat-weaving origin of sona** has no single standalone paper — Gerdes argues it inside *Sona Geometry from Angola* (origins chapter), *Lunda Geometry*, and *Geometry From Africa* (MAA, 1999; [overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometry_From_Africa)). Accessible summary: [Science News, "Sand Drawings and Mirror Curves"](https://www.sciencenews.org/article/sand-drawings-and-mirror-curves).
- ✓ **Complete bibliography of Gerdes' African-mathematics books:** [Buffalo MAD list](https://www.math.buffalo.edu/mad/special/gerdes-african-math-books.html).
- ✓ **On Gerdes (1952–2014):** [ASC Leiden obituary](https://www.ascleiden.nl/news/obituary-professor-paulus-gerdes) · [International Symmetry Association obituary](https://symmetry.hu/obituary-paulus-gerdes-mathematician-and-symmetrologist-member-of-international-symmetry-association/) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paulus_Gerdes).

## 2. Mirror curves & knot theory

- ✓ **Jablan, S., Radović, L., Sazdanović, R., Zeković, A. — "Mirror-curves and knot mosaics" (arXiv:1106.3784, 2011).** Tame knot theory ≡ mirror curves ≡ knot mosaics ≡ grid diagrams; the universality result. [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.3784) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1106.3784).
- ✓ **Jablan, S. & Radović, L. (et al.) — "Knots in Art", *Symmetry* 4(2):302–328 (2012, open access CC-BY).** Survey incl. sona/Celtic/kolam. [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-8994/4/2/302) (◐ — blocks robots, URL canonical) · [PDF mirror](https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/14a0/d0cfedbd6b75378f84af9b5d9aca57dcfba9.pdf) · DOI 10.3390/sym4020302.
- ✓ **Jablan, S. — "Mirror curves" (*Visual Mathematics*).** Free, richly illustrated exposition — excellent to link from the site. [mi.sanu.ac.rs/vismath/mir/mir.htm](https://www.mi.sanu.ac.rs/vismath/mir/mir.htm).
- ◐ **Yuan, M. — "Polyomino Loops" (Medium/The Startup, Feb 2020).** ⚠ A blog essay by Matthew Yuan, **not a refereed paper** — cite as such. Poses the open problem ("is there a nice way to determine L(P) without drawing the loops?"), two conjectures, prime-polyomino decomposition. [Article](https://medium.com/swlh/polyomino-loops-fc8f6fc92c2f). *(Correct the essay if it implies a print publication.)*

## 3. Billiard knots

- ✓ **Jones, V.F.R. & Przytycki, J.H. — "Lissajous knots and billiard knots", *Banach Center Publ.* 42:145–163 (1998).** Origin of billiard knots. [EuDML](https://eudml.org/doc/208802) · [PDF (ICM Warsaw)](http://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/bcp/bcp42/bcp42112.pdf).
- ✓ **Koseleff, P.-V. & Pecker, D. — "Every knot is a billiard knot" (arXiv:1106.5600, 2011).** [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.5600) · [PDF mirror (TCD)](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/~lebed/Koseleff,%20Pecker.Every%20knot%20is%20a%20billiard%20knot.pdf).
- ✓ **Lamm, C. & Obermeyer, D. — "Billiard knots in a cylinder" (arXiv:math/9811006; JKTR 8(3), 1999).** [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9811006).
- ✓ **Przytycki, J.H. — "Symmetric knots and billiard knots" (arXiv:math/0405151, 2004).** 41-pp survey. ⚠ By Przytycki alone — easy to misattribute to Lamm. [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0405151).
- ✓ **Eisermann, M. & Lamm, C. — "Equivalence of symmetric union diagrams" (arXiv:0705.4578; JKTR 2007).** [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.4578). Companion: ◐ "Symmetric unions and ribbon knots" (*Osaka J. Math.* 45, 2008) [Project Euclid](https://projecteuclid.org/euclid.ojm/1200789356).

## 4. Arithmetic billiards (the gcd theorem's other home)

- ◐ **Steinhaus, H. — *Mathematical Snapshots* (1938; Dover 1999).** The classic gcd/lcm-via-45°-billiards snapshot. [Internet Archive (lending)](https://archive.org/details/mathematicalsnap0000stei_k1y6).
- ◐ **Perucca, A. — "Arithmetic billiards" (*Plus Magazine*, 2018).** Friendly interactive exposition. [Article](https://plus.maths.org/content/arithmetic-billiards-0). Scholarly follow-up: Perucca, dos Reis Santos & Sousa, *Recreational Mathematics Magazine* (2022, open access) [Sciendo](https://reference-global.com/article/10.2478/rmm-2022-0003) · [ORBilu](https://orbilu.uni.lu/handle/10993/43834).

## 5. Related traditions — kolam, Celtic, curve stitching

- ✓ **Ascher, M. — "The Kolam Tradition", *American Scientist* 90(1):56–61 (2002).** [Article page](https://www.americanscientist.org/article/the-kolam-tradition) (may need registration; Gale has it open) · [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/250968699_The_Kolam_Tradition).
- ◐ **Siromoney, G. et al. — kolam & array-grammar papers** ("Array Grammars and Kolam", *CGIP* 3, 1974; "Rosenfeld's cycle grammars and kolam", 1986; "Kahbi Kolam and Cycle Grammars", 1989). [dblp](https://dblp.org/pid/62/2736.html) · [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Array-Grammars-and-Kolam-Siromoney-Siromoney/41e716f3f281fea512e39938c80230cc772050bd). ⚠ The CMI memorial archive (`cmi.ac.in/gift/`) is dead — use Wayback.
- ✓ **Cromwell, P.R. — "Celtic knotwork: mathematical art", *Mathematical Intelligencer* 15(1):36–47 (1993).** Canonical mathematical treatment of interlace. [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF03025256) (paywalled; author copy circulates on ResearchGate).
- ◐ **Boole, M.E. — *The Preparation of the Child for Science* (Clarendon, 1904).** Primary source for curve stitching; public domain, on Internet Archive (search the title). Secondary: [Innes, *Endeavour* 28(1), 2004](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0160932704000158) · [profile](https://mathwomen.agnesscott.org/women/boole.htm).

## 6. Primary ethnographic sources (the Lusophone arc)

- ✗ **Fontinha, M. — *Desenhos na areia dos Quiocos do Nordeste de Angola* (IICT, Lisboa, 1983; 304 pp).** The richest photographic field corpus of Chokwe sona — in Portuguese; the key primary source for the site's Angola↔Portugal thread. Out of print: [AbeBooks (used)](https://www.abebooks.com/Desenhos-Areia-Dos-Quiocos-Nordeste-Angola/31849695958/bd). An unofficial Scribd scan exists — consult, **don't republish**. IICT legacy is now under Universidade de Lisboa; try also `memoria-africa.ua.pt` (unreachable during research — retry; likeliest legal digitization home).
- ✗ **Baumann, H. — *Lunda: Bei Bauern und Jägern in Inner-Angola* (Würfel Verlag, Berlin, 1935; 92 plates).** Earliest substantial sona documentation. Library only: [WorldCat](https://search.worldcat.org/title/Lunda-:-Bei-bauern-und-jagern-in-Inner-Angola-:-Ergebnisse-der-Angola-expedition-des-Museums-fur-volkerkunde-Berlin/oclc/8582053) · [review record](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/africa/article/abs/lunda-bei-bauern-und-jagern-in-innerangola-von-hermann-baumann-ergebnisse-der-angolaexpedition-des-museums-fur-volkerkunde-berlin-249-s-92-tafeln-u-zahl-illustrationen-1935-berlin-wurfel-verlag-rm-50/CC771831F4C30698D2A1B59F9796416A). ⚠ Baumann's NS-era politics are documented — cite with care.
- ✓ **Kubik, G. — *Tusona–Luchazi Ideographs* (2nd enlarged ed., LIT Verlag, 2006).** In print. [Publisher](https://lit-verlag.de/isbn/978-3-8258-7601-2/) · [Google Books preview](https://books.google.com/books?id=6IiclcXvZNkC).

- ◐ **Smithsonian Open Access** ([si.edu/openaccess](https://www.si.edu/openaccess)) — CC0; NMAfA holds Chokwe *objects* (masks, chairs), not sand drawings; search "Chokwe" + CC0 filter.

- ◐ **Memórias de África e do Oriente** (`memoria-africa.ua.pt`) — Portuguese digitization portal cataloguing IICT publications (Fontinha's series) — retry; unreachable during research.
