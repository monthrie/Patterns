## What is reliably in it
- João wove a full **alphabet** — A, B, C, D … (at least to X) — **each letter as a single closed loop
  ("um ciclo")**, on his hand-made **nail boards laid on squared / graph paper**, every nail placed by hand.
- He **gave the whole set away to a house/centre for blind people in Lisbon**.
- The reason: he recalls **a (blind) girl touching the woven letters and crying**, *feeling* the work —
  *"sentir o trabalho… como ver com os dedos"* (to feel the work… like seeing with the fingers). He
  realised blind people **read the letters with their fingertips**.
- He then wanted to make them **in colour too** — the blind "feel what I feel" touching the colours; the
  sighted can see them.
- A **second story**: during an island **pilgrimage** (romeiros who walk the island for eight days; he did
  it twice), a pilgrim was **killed at night by someone coming from a discotheque**. João **wove a large
  rosary with a cross and gave it to the dead man's family**.
